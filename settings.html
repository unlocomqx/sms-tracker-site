On this page

Settings ​

You can configure the app settings in the settings page.

Password ​

The password is used to protect your devices from being tracked by others.

Allow from numbers ​

You can restrict the numbers that can send SMS to your device. This is a comma separated list of numbers.

Devices ​

You can add as many devices as you want. The number is the phone number of the device you want to track.

The app will send an SMS to the device with the following format: password gps.

Then, if the password is valid, the device will send back its location in this format password gps: [latitude,longitude].

The app will then display the location on the map.
